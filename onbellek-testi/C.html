Tuition Fees and Living Costs in Hungary

Hungary, located in the heart of Europe, is a country with a rich educational history. Offering affordable education, quality living standards, and a safe environment, Hungary has become an attractive destination for international students in recent years. So, how much are university fees in Hungary? In this article, we will provide detailed information about education fees, daily living expenses, and scholarship opportunities in Hungary.

Education Costs in Hungary

Studying in Hungary is cheaper than studying in Western Europe

In Hungary, education fees vary depending on the chosen university, program, and the student’s nationality. Generally, for undergraduate programs, you can expect to pay between 3,000 – 5,000 € per year, for master’s programs, between 4,000 – 6,000 € per year, and for doctoral programs, between 6,000 – 8,000 € per year. For medical and dental programs, the annual university fee can reach up to 16,000 €.

Annual Accommodation and Living Costs in Hungary

In addition to the tuition fees mentioned above, you should also consider accommodation and living costs. University dormitories will cost in the range of €60-€400 per month including all bills. Rental rooms: €350 approx. Studio flats: €550

Total living costs are in the range of €4000-€8,000 (per year) including accommodation, bills, food, books, transportation and entertainment though this will differ depending on your individual necessities and lifestyle. Depending on which program you choose to study and where you live, expect your total costs for the year including tuition to be around €8500 – €14,000. And for medicine , dentistry around €26,000 per year of study.

Hungary’s and Europe’s Leading Universities

Hungary’s universities offer a wide range of programs at affordable prices. In the following part of our article, we cover some popular university information, their programs’s fees, and more. Learn more about Hungarian universities. and courses. 

Eötvös Loránd Üniversitesi

Eötvös Loránd Üniversitesi, one of Hungary’s most prestigious universities, offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Located in Budapest, the university provides scholarship opportunities for international students.

Debrecen Üniversitesi

Debrecen Üniversitesi, one of Hungary’s oldest and largest universities, is known for its prominence in fields such as medicine, pharmacy, engineering, and agriculture. The city of Debrecen boasts a vibrant student atmosphere and is notable for its affordable living costs.

Peç Üniversitesi

Established in 1367, Peç Üniversitesi, located in the south of Hungary, is one of the oldest universities in Hungary with a rich educational tradition, as well as one of the oldest in the world. It offers education in various fields such as medicine, engineering, arts, and humanities. The city of Pécs is culturally and historically rich, hosting many historic buildings listed on the UNESCO World Heritage List.

Average annual tuition fees for popular Hungarian universities:

Eötvös Loránd University: 4,000 – 6,000 Euros
University of Debrecen: 3,500 – 5,500 Euros
University of Pécs: 3,000 – 5,000 Euros

Scholarship Opportunities in Hungary

The Hungarian government and several universities extend scholarship opportunities to international students, providing financial support to cover either all or a portion of their tuition fees. These scholarships serve as invaluable resources, making higher education in Hungary more accessible and affordable for students from around the world. Whether you’re pursuing undergraduate or graduate studies, these scholarship programs can significantly alleviate the financial burden associated with tuition expenses. For detailed information regarding available scholarship opportunities, you can explore resources such as the Hungarian Education and Cultural Attaché, review the respective university’s website, or reach out to us directly. We’re here to guide you through the application process and help you seize these invaluable scholarship opportunities.

Hungarian Universities Departments Prices

Hungarian universities offer a wide range of department options. Some of the highly popular departments are sought after and chosen by international students, while the prices of departments at Hungarian universities can be compared with those of other European countries. Here are some price ranges for departments at Hungarian universities:

Hungarian Pilot Training: Semesterly 7,500–13,500 USD
Hungarian Medical Education: Starting from 16,500 USD annually
Hungarian Dentistry: Starting from 17,350 EUR annually
Hungarian Psychology Bachelor’s/Master’s: Starting from 7,800 EUR
Hungarian English Preparatory Education: Starting from 2,500 EUR per semester

In addition to the examples above, Hungarian universities offer numerous department options. Whether you want to study for a bachelor’s or master’s degree in your desired university department, you can easily learn about Hungarian universities’ department prices, complete your application with Hun Education, and become a valued student at a Hungarian university.

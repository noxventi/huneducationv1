Why should you study in Hungary?

Studying at a university in Hungary is one of the most accessible routes to a degree from an EU member state, taught entirely in English. Universities do not ask for a national entrance exam score; each faculty runs its own assessment. Tuition and living costs together come to €8,500 – €14,000 for an academic year. Long-established Hungarian universities such as Semmelweis, Debrecen, Pécs and Szeged teach medicine, engineering, business, master’s degrees and pilot training in English.

Hun Education has focused on this one country since 1999. We match students to the right option among 20 universities and 490 English-taught programmes. We go through current university fees in Hungary and the admission requirements with you before you apply. The full case for the country, with the figures behind it, is on the Why Hungary? page.

The process with Hun Education

From the first call to your first week in Hungary

You work with the same team through all six steps. What is expected of you and what we do at each step is set out in writing in advance.

  1. 01

    HOME COUNTRY

    Free consultation and goal analysis

    We talk through your academic record, your English level, your budget and what you want after graduation. By the end of the call we tell you which levels and which fields are realistic options for you.

    Your part
    Have your transcript and English level ready
    Our part
    We produce a realistic shortlist and a timeline
    • 30–45 min
    • Online or in office
    • Free
  2. 02

    HOME COUNTRY

    Matching universities and programmes

    We put the programmes that match your profile side by side with their admission conditions, fees, city and application deadlines. We also explain why any programme was ruled out.

    Your part
    Settle on your city and budget
    Our part
    A comparison table and an admission-conditions analysis
    • Comparison shortlist
    • Admission analysis
  3. 03

    HOME COUNTRY

    Preparing the documents

    We work through a checklist: apostilled diploma, English transcript, passport, English CV and a six-month bank statement. The apostille stage is where most time is lost.

    Your part
    Book the apostille and sworn translation appointments
    Our part
    We run the checklist and audit the file
    • Document checklist
    • Apostille guidance
  4. 04

    HOME COUNTRY

    Application, entrance exam and offer

    We submit through the university’s own system. Where there is a chemistry–biology exam for health programmes or a physics–mathematics exam for engineering, we plan your preparation. On a positive result you receive a conditional offer first and the final Acceptance Letter after payment.

    Your part
    Prepare for the entrance exam or interview
    Our part
    The application, exam follow-up and offer correspondence
    • April–June for autumn
    • Exam preparation
  5. 05

    HOME COUNTRY → HUNGARY

    Visa, accommodation and travel

    You apply at your nearest Hungarian consulate with the final Acceptance Letter. We research dormitory and rental options around your offer. The visa decision rests with the consulate; if the visa is refused, tuition is refunded within 30 working days.

    Your part
    Attend the consulate appointment
    Our part
    The document file and the accommodation search
    • Type D student visa
    • Dormitory or rental
  6. 06

    HUNGARY

    Arrival and orientation

    Our Budapest team is with you for the airport pick-up, residence registration, bank account, transport card and university enrolment. You keep the same adviser throughout your studies.

    Your part
    Share your flight details with us
    Our part
    Pick-up, residence and enrolment support
    • Airport pick-up
    • Residence & enrolment

The scope of each step can vary with the service package you choose. The admission decision rests with the university, the visa decision with the relevant consulate and the recognition decision with your national authority; Hun Education does not guarantee those outcomes.

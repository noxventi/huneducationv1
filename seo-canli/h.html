Application Requirements

  • High School Graduation: To apply for university in Hungary, you only need to have a high school diploma. It doesn’t matter which high school you graduated from in Turkey or your field of study.

  • No Exam Requirement: There is no need to take official university exams when applying to Hungarian universities. However, if you are applying to universities outside the top 1000, you may need to take local university exams for equivalency purposes.

  • English Language Proficiency: If you have proof of your English language proficiency, such as IELTS or TOEFL test results, or if you have graduated from an English-language school, you can directly start university without a preparatory year.

  • Preparatory Education: Nevertheless, it is advisable to consider preparatory education before starting your university journey in Hungary. This education helps you become familiar with European culture, and the EU education system, and contributes to your English language skills. It also allows you to study core science subjects in English, enhancing your readiness for university success.

Application Process

  • Selecting the right university and course is your first step toward your dream education, and we’re here to guide you through the process.
  • Begin by exploring the wide range of courses available, and you can access more detailed information about costs of universities.
  • Before applying, conduct an admissions review to ensure you meet program-specific criteria, including age limits, language requirements, and entrance exams, if applicable. 
  • Simplify the application process by allowing us to apply to your chosen university on your behalf once you’ve made your decisions.
  • Prepare the necessary documents, including your high school diploma, proof of English language proficiency (if studying in an English program), a copy of your passport, a motivation letter, and a curriculum vitae (CV). You should be aware that some universities may require an application fee, the amount of which varies between institutions.
  • Certain programs, particularly in fields like medicine, engineering, or the arts, may necessitate specific entrance exams. Make sure to research and prepare accordingly.
  • Upon a thorough review of your application, you will receive an acceptance letter confirming your admission and any accompanying conditions.
  • After securing this confirmation, the next step is to apply for a student visa to study in Hungary. 
  • Lastly, organize your travel arrangements to Hungary and secure suitable accommodation ahead of your departure for a seamless transition into your educational journey.

You can submit your application online or by mail. Once you’ve completed your application, you can use the application tracking process to receive regular updates about the status of your application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I study in Hungary?

Hungary, due to its geographical location in Europe and the diversity of universities and programs, has become a top choice for international education. Hungarian universities offer high-quality education with a rich history. Hungary stands out as a country with a more affordable cost of living compared to other European countries. By studying in Hungary, you can gain international experience and explore different cultures.

Do diplomas from Hungarian universities have recognition from YÖK (Turkish Higher Education Council) and international equivalency?

Yes, diplomas from many Hungarian universities are recognized by the Turkish Higher Education Council (YÖK) and have international validity. This means that you can work anywhere after graduating from a Hungarian university.

What are the accommodation options in Hungary?

 Hungarian universities offer affordable accommodation options. You can comfortably stay in shared dormitories for 2 or 3 people during your university education in Hungary. Apart from dormitories, there are student apartments and other affordable housing options located near your university. As Hun Education, we help you determine your accommodation during your university education.

What are the advantages of studying in Hungary?

Firstly, Hungarian Universities have international recognition. Studying in Hungary means you can build your career anywhere afterwards. Moreover, Hungarian universities offer high-quality education. This allows you to graduate with full competence. English and German language options improve your language skills, giving you an advantage after graduation. Compared to Turkey and other European countries, Hungary offers more affordable tuition fees for education and lower living costs. Being centrally located in Europe, you can easily travel from Hungary to other countries.

Is it possible to work while studying in Hungary?

 International students studying in Hungarian universities are allowed to work part-time. Part-time work opportunities, in line with your program, provide students studying in Hungary with additional income opportunities. This way, you won’t face financial difficulties and can easily cover your expenses. Additionally, you’ll enrich yourself socially and culturally and improve your language skills.

What are the universities in Budapest?

Budapest, as the capital and largest city of Hungary, is home to several universities. Among these universities are Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE), Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME),  Semmelweis University, Corvinus University of Budapest, Budapest Metropolitan University, and many others. These institutions offer a wide range of educational and research opportunities across various disciplines and cater to international students with diverse academic interests. Budapest, renowned for its historical and cultural significance, provides a rich academic environment enriched by these universities.

What is the visa application process for studying in Hungary?

You can easily apply for a visa with Hun Education’s support and consultancy. We assist you in this process. After choosing your university and undergraduate or graduate programs in Hungary, we initiate your university application. The necessary documents for Hungarian universities are collected from you, and your university application is made. After that, you can apply for a student visa to study in Hungary once you complete the Hungarian university admissions process.

What fields of study are available in Hungary?

Hungarian universities offer undergraduate and postgraduate programs in a wide range of fields, including medicine, dentistry, health sciences, engineering, business, arts, and many more. Almost all universities serve as the official application center for you. You can apply to Hungarian university programs in undergraduate or graduate fields that are completely compatible with your career goals.

How much are the tuition fees for universities in Hungary?

Tuition fees at Hungarian universities may vary depending on the program and the university. Generally, they offer the opportunity to receive education at affordable tuition fees. Programs like medicine and dentistry start at $16,000, while other undergraduate and postgraduate program fees start at $3,500 per year. By studying in Hungary, you can receive high-quality education at a more affordable cost compared to other countries.

What are the university entrance exams like in Hungary?

Hungarian universities are independent of the university entrance exams in Turkey, such as YKS (Higher Education Institutions Exam). Universities conduct interviews or written examinations related to the field of study. These exams are generally straightforward and passable.

What is the English language requirement for Hungarian Universities?

Hungarian universities require you to meet certain entry requirements. Some Hungarian universities require proof of your English language proficiency through exams like IELTS, DUOLINGO, and others. Don’t worry; Hungarian universities also offer preparatory programs that can help you meet the language requirement even if your proficiency is insufficient. Additionally, English preparatory programs in Hungary are quite affordable and provide high-quality education.

What are the most popular departments at Hungarian universities?

Hungary offers a wide range of academic programs for international students, making it easy for them to find their desired field of study at Hungarian universities, whether they are pursuing an undergraduate or graduate degree. Popular fields of study among international students in Hungary include medicine, engineering, business, natural sciences, and social sciences. One of the advantages of studying in Hungary is that many universities offer programs taught in English, which makes education more accessible to international students. Additionally, Hungarian universities are internationally recognized, providing students with an excellent starting point for advancing their careers. In conclusion, there is a diverse range of options for international students interested in studying in Hungary, allowing them to easily find a program that suits their interests.

What Research Opportunities Do Hungarian Universities Offer?

Hungarian universities offer various research opportunities in a wide range of fields. These opportunities encompass science and technology, medicine and health sciences, social sciences, natural sciences, literature and the arts, as well as energy and environmental technologies. Universities in Hungary actively engage in international projects and provide access to extensive resources and state-of-the-art laboratory facilities. Engaging in research at Hungarian universities provides valuable international exposure and contributes to one’s career development, making these institutions significant centers for those looking to expand their research horizons.

What Student Discounts and Support are Available in Hungary?

In Hungary, various discounts and support mechanisms are available to help students manage their living costs. Particularly, students can benefit from significant discounts on local transportation services, making inner-city travel more affordable. Additionally, students with their identification cards often gain access to cultural events, theater performances, museum visits, and concerts at reduced rates. Some universities in Hungary offer meal discounts or provide cost-effective accommodation options in student dormitories. These discounts and support systems aim to reduce the financial burden on international students and enable them to fully engage in cultural experiences while studying in Hungary.
